AI Visibility Consulting for Distilleries

When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Google’s AI to recommend a bourbon, gin, or whiskey, it names a few distilleries and skips the rest. I make sure yours is one it names.

Short answer: AI visibility consulting for distilleries makes your spirits brand the name an assistant recommends when a buyer asks for a bourbon, gin, or whiskey. More of your buyers are skipping the search bar. They open an assistant and type something specific, “a smooth bourbon under $50,” “a good gin for a Negroni,” “which craft distilleries ship to Illinois,” and they trust the distilleries the AI names back. Getting into that answer is a specific, winnable game. It has surprisingly little to do with what’s in the bottle, and a lot to do with whether a machine can read and verify you.

Why distilleries get skipped

The distilleries an assistant names are the ones easiest for it to read and corroborate. Most spirits brands lose on exactly the things a distillery tends to get wrong:

  • The specs are trapped in PDFs and images. Category, proof, mash bill, cask, and tasting notes live in a beautiful sell sheet a machine can barely read. If you can’t highlight it with your cursor, the AI can’t either.
  • The name isn’t consistent. One spelling on your site, another on a retailer, a third on a review site, and the machine can’t merge them into one trusted distillery.
  • There’s no outside corroboration. An assistant checks reviews, ratings, and the press before it vouches for you. Awards listed only on your own site count for little.
  • An age gate is blocking the crawlers. A full-screen “Are you 21?” wall can hide the whole page from the readers that feed AI answers.

What I do

I make your distillery legible to machines without losing what makes it worth choosing. The work is concrete:

  • An audit of what search engines and AI say about your distillery today, and the specific gaps keeping you out of answers.
  • Machine-readable pages: your real specs, category, proof, mash bill, cask, serve suggestions, and where to buy, as text on the page.
  • Structured data that labels your distillery, products, and reviews so an assistant can quote you with confidence instead of guessing.
  • One consistent name across your site, retail listings, and reviews, so every mention reinforces one entity.
  • A plan to build the third-party footprint AI trusts, on the sources buyers already check.
