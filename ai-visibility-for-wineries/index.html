AI Visibility Consulting for Wineries

When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Google’s AI to recommend a wine, it names a few wineries and skips the rest. I make sure yours is one it names.

Short answer: AI visibility consulting for wineries makes your winery the name an assistant recommends when a buyer asks for a wine. More of your buyers are skipping the search bar. They open an assistant and type something specific, “a good Willamette Pinot under $40,” “which Paso wineries ship to Texas,” “a wine gift for a Cabernet lover,” and they trust the wineries the AI names back. Getting into that answer is a specific, winnable game. It has surprisingly little to do with what’s in the bottle, and a lot to do with whether a machine can read and verify you.

Why wineries get skipped

The wineries an assistant names are the ones easiest for it to read and corroborate. Most wineries lose on exactly the things a wine business tends to get wrong:

  • The facts are trapped in PDFs and images. Tasting notes, tech sheets, and the story live in beautiful downloads a machine can barely read. If you can’t highlight it with your cursor, the AI can’t either.
  • The name isn’t consistent. One spelling on your site, another on Vivino, a third on a distributor listing, and the machine can’t merge them into one trusted winery.
  • There’s no outside corroboration. An assistant checks Vivino, Wine-Searcher, and the press before it vouches for you. Praise on your own site counts for little.
  • An age gate is blocking the crawlers. A full-screen “Are you 21?” wall can hide the whole page from the readers that feed AI answers.

What I do

I make your winery legible to machines without losing what makes it worth choosing. The work is concrete:

  • An audit of what search engines and AI say about your winery today, and the specific gaps keeping you out of answers.
  • Machine-readable pages: your real facts, occasion and pairing language, shipping states, and tasting notes as text on the page.
  • Structured data that labels your winery, wines, and reviews so an assistant can quote you with confidence instead of guessing.
  • One consistent name across your site, listings, and reviews, so every mention reinforces one entity.
  • A plan to build the third-party footprint AI trusts, on the sources buyers already check.
