Getting found by AI: the questions people ask

Plain-English answers on how AI search works and how to make your business the one it names — from how AI Overviews pick their sources to what the work costs and how long it takes.

How AI search works

How does Google’s AI Overview work?

Google’s AI Overview reads the top results for a query, pulls the passages it judges most relevant and trustworthy, and stitches them into a short written answer with links to its sources. It isn’t ranking pages so much as quoting them — so the goal shifts from “rank #1” to “be the clearest, most trustworthy passage on the question.”

Where does AI Overview get its information?

From the live web — mostly pages that already rank well, community sources it trusts like Reddit, Quora, and YouTube, and structured data that spells out what a page is. If your answer to a question is clear, well-sourced, and machine-readable, you’re eligible to be one of the passages it quotes.

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO earns a ranked link on a results page. GEO — generative engine optimization — earns a mention inside the AI’s written answer. They share the same foundations (clear content, structured data, authority), but GEO adds machine-facing signals aimed at being quoted, not just linked. The Playbook walks through all eight.

Getting your business found

How do I get AI to recognize my business?

Give the machine something clean to read and reasons to trust it: put your key facts on the page as real text, use one consistent name everywhere, add structured data, and earn mentions on sources the AI already reads. AI recognizes the businesses it can clearly read and verify. The six-step walkthrough covers each move.

How do I make my business appear in AI searches?

Publish pages whose headline is the question a buyer asks and whose first sentence is the answer, keep them factual and skimmable, add structured data, and make sure AI crawlers aren’t blocked. The clearer and more trustworthy the passage, the likelier the AI quotes it.

How do I get AI to mention my business by name?

A by-name mention takes the machine-readable basics plus authority — press, credible reviews, and a consistent presence across the platforms in your category. It’s earned trust, not a setting you switch on. COLAClear earned one in Google’s AI Overview with zero ad spend.

How do I get my business to show up in ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers from what it can read about you across the web and, when browsing, from live pages. Clear on-page text, one consistent name, structured data, open crawler access, and credible third-party mentions all raise the odds it names you. The quickest test: ask it to recommend a business like yours and see if you appear.

How do I show up in Google’s AI Overviews?

The same content and authority that earn a page-one ranking make you eligible to be quoted in an AI Overview — there’s no ad for it. Answer a specific question directly and early on the page, mark it up with structured data, and make sure the page is crawlable.

How long does it take to get found by AI?

The technical fixes — text, structured data, crawler access, consistent naming — can be done in days and get picked up as pages are re-crawled over the following weeks. Authority, the press and mentions that earn by-name citations, is a months-long build. Anyone promising instant AI visibility is selling something.
